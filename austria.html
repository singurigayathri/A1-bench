Two bases differ and the gap is large enough to matter. Deutsche Telekom reports adjusted EBITDA AL, which strips special factors; A1's segment EBITDAaL carries EUR 96 mn of Austrian restructuring in 2025. A1's Austrian revenue also includes roughly EUR 155 mn of low-margin international transit that Magenta does not carry. The like-for-like toggle corrects both. Magenta's 2025 figure is nine months, not a full year — the margin is comparable, the absolute revenue is not.

Filling the Drei gap

  • Hutchison Drei Austria files annual accounts in the Austrian Firmenbuch — revenue, operating result and headcount, under UGB rather than IFRS, with up to a year's lag.
  • RTR publishes subscriber counts and market shares for all three operators from one consistent source. This is the only genuinely like-for-like operational data in the market.
  • CK Hutchison's group reporting covers its European telecoms division; Austria is not consistently broken out.
